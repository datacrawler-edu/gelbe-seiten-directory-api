# FAQ

## Can I use a postcode?

Yes. Set `location` to a German postcode. Use a city name when selecting city districts.

## Why are opening hours empty?

Enable `includeDetails` to request full schedules. A business may have no published hours.

## Why did I receive fewer listings than the cap?

The cap is a maximum. Check the source coverage and the `SUMMARY` stop reason.

## What is billed?

The Actor charges per unique listing saved. Check the [Actor Pricing tab](https://apify.com/datascraperes/gelbe-seiten-business-directory?fpr=edudata) for the current rates.
